1/5
PibloCraft is in early access.

It runs your coding agents side by side in split terminal panes, gives them a job, and shows you what needs you.

piblo.co.uk/piblocraft

2/5
Pick an agent for each pane. Claude (two accounts), Codex, Hermes, or one of 20 other providers like DeepSeek, Kimi and OpenRouter.

Each pane shows how much usage is left and stars the best one.

3/5
Give it a job in plain words:

pc "Improve the README"

Or a bounded shift: "for 2 hours". Work runs in separate git worktrees. In the morning, pc morning tells you what happened.

4/5
The part I use most is the status line.

pc ? says who is working, who is waiting for you, and what is open. pc answer walks through the decisions one at a time.

5/5
It is quiet by default. Nothing starts on its own, a guard blocks risky commands (a safety net, not a sandbox), and your keys stay on your PC.

Windows today. Code is private for now.

Early access: piblo.co.uk/piblocraft

          How work flows

          Four steps, whichever AI tools you use.

          1. 1

            Give it a task

            Type what you want done. PibloCraft picks the agents for the job.

          2. 2

            Watch the work

            Each agent says what it’s doing in plain words, not terminal output.

          3. 3

            Make the decisions

            Anything that changes things comes back to you: approve, change or deny.

          4. 4

            Pick up later

            Tomorrow, you’re not starting over. Handoff summaries (coming soon) say where to resume.

          What’s under the hood?
          • Your choice of AI. Use cloud models such as Claude and Codex alongside free models that run on your own PC. PibloCraft can send each job to the one that suits it.
          • Piblo Core for Windows is the small companion app that keeps the shared task list on your computer.
          • Approvals stay with you. Actions that publish, delete or spend wait for your answer.

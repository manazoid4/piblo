Piblo timeline

Where we are and what comes next. We only add a date when it is honest. Until then you get real progress, in order.

  1. Working

    A working prototype

    Piblo connects to a computer, shows its screen there and saves a spoken note.

  2. In testing

    Testing it for real

    Making the voice quicker and more natural, improving the sound, and checking nudges, focus sessions, voice typing and Ask on Piblo on a real device.

  3. Planned

    A small group of early testers

    A handful of people try it in daily life and tell us what to change.

  4. Planned

    Kickstarter

    Only when the prototype is proven and the costs are checked. The launch list hears first.

  5. Planned

    Making it

    Samples, checks and a first production run, with updates along the way.

  6. Planned

    In your hands

    Delivery to backers, then add-ons and updates.

Concepts and ideas on this site are not promises. Plans can change as we test.

03STAGES

None of this is promised. It's what we're aiming for.

NOWWorks on the desk

Piblo pairs with a computer over Wi-Fi. Press and hold a key, say a thought, and it lands in your notes. You can pick the AI you like. Tested on our prototype.

TESTINGVoice, nudges and Ask on Piblo

You can ask a question and hear an answer. It works, but replies are still slow. Leave-now nudges, focus sessions, voice typing and Show me once are built and being checked on the device. So is Ask on Piblo: before anything is sent or booked, Piblo will ask you first.

NEXTHelpers, catch-ups and Rescue

Little helpers you ask for by voice, a two-minute catch-up, sharing from your phone, Private mode, Buddy's six forms and Piblo Rescue. Then uncut videos of the prototype doing everyday jobs.

01How it works

Three small steps, then back to your day.

  1. 1

    Tell it once

    Type a thought, a job or a time on real keys. No app to open, no window to find.

  2. 2

    Piblo keeps it

    It becomes a note, a reminder or a focus timer, saved on your own PC.

  3. 3

    It nudges you

    A small nudge at the right moment. Then it goes quiet again.

08Questions

Straight answers.

What works today?

A working prototype that keeps notes, reminders, focus timers and nudges, with a Windows companion app in working beta. The Android app, voice conversations and Piblo Flow are in testing and not shipped.

Do I need a computer?

Yes. Piblo sits beside a Windows PC and works with the Piblo app running on it. An Android phone app is in testing.

Is it private?

Piblo is local-first: it runs on your own PC, and the core works without an account.

Can I talk to it?

Not yet. Voice conversations and Piblo Flow (hold a key, speak, and the text appears in any Windows app) are in testing.

How much will it cost?

The price is not announced. Two editions are planned, Standard and Pro, and the details are not final. The launch list will hear first.

When is the Kickstarter?

It is planned, with no date announced yet. Join the launch list and we will email you when it opens.

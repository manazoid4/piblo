Questions

Straight answers

What works today?

A working prototype: an M5Stack Cardputer handheld talking to Piblo Core on a Windows PC. Reminders, promises, focus sessions and approvals run there today. Piblo Home and the final pocket piece are being designed and printed now.

How does the £5 reservation work?

It’s refundable any time before shipping: reply to your receipt or email info@piblo.co.uk. It isn’t a deposit taken off the price. It gives you priority access and the £129 early-price offer.

Why not just use my phone?

For a timer, use your phone. Piblo is for things that take several steps there: acting on what’s open on your PC, holding you to promises, and approving actions with a key that can’t be pressed remotely.

Does it need a computer?

Today, yes: Piblo Core runs on a Windows PC. Phone support is planned, starting with Android.

Is it always listening?

No. You press a key to talk. Nothing is recorded in the background.

Is the code open?

The source is public on GitHub and free for personal, non-commercial use (PolyForm Noncommercial).

When does it ship?

No date yet. We’ll set one when the hardware is proven and quotes are in.

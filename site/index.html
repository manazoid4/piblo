06Questions

Straight answers

Why not just use my phone?

Your phone can do a lot of this. Piblo is a calm, separate place on your desk: real keys, a screen that stays in view, and no feed to fall into.

Is it always listening?

No. You press a key to talk. It’s local-first and needs no account.

Will it actually ship?

No date or price yet. We post dated progress on the timeline, marked done, at risk or late, so you can judge for yourself.

Do I need to code?

No. Install the Windows app, pair, press a key. Setup is still in beta and we’re timing it with real people.

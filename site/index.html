01How it works

How Piblo works

  1. 1

    Say it

    “Remind me to call Mum after work.” Talk naturally, or press the mic key.

  2. 2

    It handles it

    Piblo remembers, sets things up and will put little helpers to work. Before anything that matters, it asks you first.

  3. 3

    It speaks up

    “Your train’s delayed. Leave ten minutes early.” Only when it matters, then it goes quiet again.

What you need: a phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi running the Piblo app (a phone app is planned). Cloud AI is optional. Windows app is in testing. Android comes next, then Linux, macOS and iPhone.

06Questions

Straight answers

What works today?

On our prototype we have tested pairing with a Windows computer over Wi-Fi, saving a spoken note to your notes there, and choosing which AI it uses. Voice questions work, but answers are still slow. Nudges, focus, voice typing and Ask on Piblo are built and being checked on the device. Every feature on this page carries an honest label.

Does it need a computer?

Today, yes: the prototype pairs with a Windows computer. The plan is that a phone will be enough for everyday reminders and notes, with a computer giving you the best voice. Android comes next, then Linux, macOS and iPhone.

Does it work offline?

It needs your home or office Wi-Fi to reach your phone or computer. Reminders and notes are designed to stay on your own devices, without the internet. Spoken answers need a speech or AI service, which is usually online unless you run one yourself.

Can an AI do something without asking me?

Reading, summarising and reminding just happen. Anything that sends, books or posts is designed to wait for you: Piblo will show who, what and how much on its own screen, then wait for your yes. That part is still in testing. Payments and deletions are not built yet.

How is it different from my phone?

It does a few things and nothing else. No apps, no feeds, no notifications you didn’t ask for. It shows one thing at a time and you answer with a real key, so you can deal with it and get back to your day.

How much will it cost?

Price to be announced. We’ll share it with the launch list first. Shipping will be extra.

When does the Kickstarter start, and when would it ship?

There is no date yet. We’ll only set one when the prototype is proven and the costs are checked, and backers will get an honest ship estimate based on real manufacturing progress.

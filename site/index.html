02 · Why not your phone?

Fewer steps than unlocking your phone.

Your phone can set a reminder. It can’t see the spreadsheet you’re in, save it and hold you to it in one sentence, without pulling you into a feed.

Approval

A key, not a pop-up.

Anything that changes your PC, sends or books waits for a physical press. A message from the internet can’t fake it; nothing runs if you ignore it.

Privacy

Your notes live on your PC.

Reminders, notes and memory are plain files on your own computer. No account is needed to pair. Cloud AI is optional and switchable.

Focus

One thing at a time.

No feed, no app store, no badges. It shows one card and you answer with a key, then get back to your day.

06 · Questions

Straight answers

What works today?

A working prototype: an M5Stack Cardputer handheld talking to Piblo Core on a Windows PC. Reminders, promises, focus sessions and approvals run there today, and the two runs above were recorded on it. Piblo Home and the final pocket piece are designs, not built yet.

What’s in Batch 01?

The proposed bundle is Piblo Standard (the pocket piece) plus Piblo Home (the desk base), at a £129 early price. Design, parts and price can still change before the campaign.

How does the £5 reservation work?

It’s refundable any time before shipping: reply to your receipt or email info@piblo.co.uk. It’s separate from the price, not a deposit taken off it. It gives you priority access and the £129 early-price offer.

Why not just use my phone?

For a timer, use your phone. Piblo is for the things that take several steps on a phone: acting on what’s open on your PC, holding you to promises, and approving actions with a key that can’t be pressed remotely, without pulling you into notifications.

Does it need a computer?

Today, yes: Piblo Core runs on a Windows PC and does the heavy work. Phone support is planned, starting with Android.

Can an AI do something without asking me?

Reading, summarising and reminding just happen. Anything that changes your PC, sends, books or posts waits for YES on Piblo, shows what it’s about to do, and expires if you don’t answer. Payments are not built.

Is it always listening?

No. You press a key to talk. Nothing is recorded in the background.

Is the code open?

The source is public on GitHub and free for personal, non-commercial use (PolyForm Noncommercial). It’s not an OSI open-source licence.

When does the Kickstarter start, and when would it ship?

No date yet. We’ll set one when the hardware is proven and quotes are in, and backers will get an honest ship estimate based on real manufacturing progress.
